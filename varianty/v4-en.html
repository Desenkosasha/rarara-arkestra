A team with no musical background starts playing together within a few hours

In sound you immediately hear what a meeting hides: who leads, who listens, who carries the group. From there it becomes a conversation about how the team really works.

04Who leads

Dima Ustinov

Dima Ustinov

Musician, composer and sound artist. For years he has been assembling ensembles from people who have never played music.

He created the augmented piano — an acoustic grand connected to electronics and light. He works with how sound and shared action create connection between people and shift the state of a group.

his viewI'm interested in how music can heal — both a person, and what happens between people
